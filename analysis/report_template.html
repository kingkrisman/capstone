    Capacity worth protecting

    Fuel costs about $0.60 a mile on every lane, so the rate charged is what separates a good lane from a poor one. Long cross-country lanes paying under $1.85 a mile leave little room for driver pay, insurance and overhead. Net is revenue left after estimated fuel.
